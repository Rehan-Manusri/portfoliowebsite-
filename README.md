# 🌟Rehan Mansuri - Personal Portfolio Website

An ultra-premium, modern, and interactive developer portfolio website built for **Mrunali Patil** (Artificial Intelligence & Data Science Engineering Student at R.C. Patel Institute of Technology, Shirpur).

---

## 💎 Premium Design & Architecture Highlights

1. **Modern Visual Aesthetics**:
   - **Dynamic Cursor Spotlight**: Every card reflects a subtle radial light beam that tracks the visitor's cursor (`--mouse-x`, `--mouse-y`).
   - **Atmospheric Lighting**: Deep cosmic dark palette (`#05070e`), glassmorphic cards (`backdrop-blur-xl`), and ambient glowing gradient orbs.
   - **Tactile Micro-Grid**: Subtle geometric background dot matrix with radial vignetting.
   - **Button Shimmer & Badges**: Fluid light sweep animations on primary action buttons and glowing status badges.

2. **Hero Section Showcase**:
   - **Dynamic Typewriter**: Continuously cycles through core engineering roles (*AI & Data Science Student*, *Aspiring ML Developer*, *Creator of AI Study Buddy*, etc.).
   - **Interactive Developer Terminal**:
     - Modeled after a macOS/UNIX terminal with colored window controls.
     - Interactive file tab switcher (`Profile.py`, `TechStack.json`, `AI_Buddy.py`) that lets visitors view real syntax-highlighted code.
     - Live badges: `FY CGPA: 6.50`, `Cybersecurity Focus`, `Open for Internships`.

3. **About Me - Modern Bento Grid**:
   - 5-cell high-density Bento Grid highlighting Mrunali's engineering journey, academic institution (RCPIT, Shirpur), 10th grade distinction (92.80%), core AI interests, and cybersecurity curiosity.

4. **Featured Project: Interactive "AI Study Buddy" Simulator**:
   - Brings Mrunali's concept to life with an interactive simulation interface.
   - Visitors can click sample student queries (*Explain Recursion*, *Java Encapsulation*, *Cybersecurity Basics*, *C Arrays*) to test the AI assistant with real-time streaming answers.

5. **Practical Problem-Solving Labs**:
   - Deep-dive cards for **C Programming Practice**, **Python Logic Practice**, and **Java OOP Architecture**.
   - Interactive category filter buttons (`All`, `AI & Concept`, `Languages`).

6. **Technical Skills Matrix**:
   - Visual fluency bars and structured breakdowns across **Programming Languages**, **AI & Data Science**, **Cybersecurity**, and **Problem Solving**.

7. **Academic Timeline & Milestones**:
   - Illuminated vertical journey tracking her Bachelor of Engineering at RCPIT and Secondary School Certificate at R.C. Patel Junior College.

8. **Interactive Connect & Resume Hub**:
   - One-click copy email button with instant checkmark feedback and toast notification.
   - Working contact message form simulation with validation.
   - Instant printable resume snapshot modal with browser print (`Ctrl + P`) support.
